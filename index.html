Correcting an issued certificate

A certificate is never edited. The original is voided with a reason on the record, and a replacement is issued referencing it. Both stay verifiable forever — that is what makes the document worth anything to an auditor.

The voided certificate stays in the register, struck through, with the reason attached.

Certificate lookup

A client or their auditor enters the certificate number and sees whether it is current, superseded, or void. The record integrity check runs behind it — if a weight or date were altered after issue, the lookup fails.
